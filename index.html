Relationships:

Generalization: Doctor is a kind of User (Administrator, Lab Tech, Pharmacist also inherit User).
Composition: Patient has 1..* MedicalRecords (a record has no meaning without its patient).
Association: Doctor updates MedicalRecord (1 to *); Patient pays Bills (1 to *).
Dependency: Bill depends on MedicalRecord for tests and medicines billed.
